Curation and Forma

I helped curate The House America Built at the Orange County Museum of Art and led its title-wall design. I also built Forma, a tool for planning exhibitions in 3D.

The House America Built

Exhibition curation · Title-wall design

As part of OCMA’s Curator Intern Program, I worked with fellow student curators on The House America Built. I led the title-wall design for the exhibition’s June 2026 opening.

The wall needed to carry the exhibition title, interpretive text, and credits at gallery scale. I made three measured layout studies to work through the type and spacing. We chose the second direction.

Forma

3D exhibition planning · Swift

Forma is an iOS and iPadOS app I designed and built in Swift. Curators can arrange artworks in a virtual gallery, measure placement, and inspect a layout in perspective, plan, or elevation view.

The tool has been used by more than 25 curators at OCMA and the Moulton Museum. It was selected as a winner of Apple’s Swift Student Challenge.
