Atlas

Zero Abuse Project · Product design

I was the sole designer on the 0→1 development of Atlas, an intelligence platform designed to help DOJ investigators identify emerging patterns in child abuse.

Investigators work through a vast amount of information. Atlas helps them find emerging patterns, examine the research behind each pattern, and trace findings back to their sources.

From search to source

Research workflow

Search brings topics, platforms, populations, and sources into one starting point. A pattern page then holds its summary, review state, supporting items, and a timeline of sources. Investigators can open an item to inspect the evidence behind the pattern.

The evidence stays visible

Pattern · Item · Source

Building the research record

Add material · Access · Sources

The same system supports the work around a finding: adding a new research item, reviewing who can access the workspace, and checking source records when a crawl fails.

Inside the Figma file

Screen organization

I organized the Figma file around the main paths through Atlas: home and search, emerging patterns, and reviewing a pattern. The canvas shows how the screens connect.
