Nobel Week

The Daily Californian · Data Developer

I pitched and led a scrollytelling feature for UC Berkeley’s Nobel Week, designing and developing interactive visualizations for 63 laureates with a reporting partner.

The story moves between portraits, prize categories, decades, and the places where laureates worked. Readers can select a portrait to see a person’s profile without leaving the visual story.
